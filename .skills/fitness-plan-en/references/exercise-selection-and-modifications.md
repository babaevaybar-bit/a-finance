# Exercise Selection and Modifications

Use this reference to choose exercises by movement pattern, equipment, goal, and constraints. Prefer exercises the user can perform safely and consistently.

## Movement Patterns

Build balanced plans from these patterns:

- Squat: knee-dominant lower body.
- Hinge: hip-dominant lower body.
- Horizontal push.
- Vertical push.
- Horizontal pull.
- Vertical pull.
- Lunge or single-leg.
- Core: anti-extension, anti-rotation, flexion, carry.
- Locomotion or conditioning.

Not every session needs every pattern, but the week should be balanced.

## Beginner-Friendly Defaults

Use when the user is new or returning:

- Full-body 2 to 3 days/week.
- 4 to 6 exercises/session.
- 2 to 3 sets/exercise.
- 6 to 12 reps for strength movements.
- 8 to 15 reps for accessories.
- RPE 6 to 8.
- Avoid training to failure.
- Keep exercises stable for 4 to 6 weeks.

## Gym Workout Options

Machines are useful for:

- Beginners learning patterns.
- Hypertrophy with lower coordination demand.
- Training around minor limitations.
- Stable progression.

Free weights are useful for:

- Strength skill.
- Coordination.
- Flexible progression.
- Compound training.

Blend both when possible.

## Pain-Aware Modifications

General rule:

- Sharp pain: stop the movement.
- Joint pain that worsens across sets: modify or stop.
- Muscle effort, mild burn, or normal fatigue: usually acceptable.
- Persistent pain after training: reduce volume/intensity and consider professional assessment.

Knee sensitivity:

- Try box squat, leg press with controlled range, step-up, hip hinge, glute bridge.
- Reduce depth or load temporarily.
- Avoid painful jumping or high-impact conditioning.

Lower-back sensitivity:

- Try goblet squat, split squat, hip thrust, chest-supported row, machines.
- Reduce axial loading.
- Avoid heavy deadlifts if pain worsens.
- Emphasize bracing and hinge pattern practice.

Shoulder sensitivity:

- Try neutral-grip dumbbell press, landmine press, incline push-up, cable row.
- Avoid painful overhead work.
- Balance pushing with pulling.

Wrist sensitivity:

- Use dumbbells or handles for push-ups.
- Choose neutral grips.
- Avoid loaded extended-wrist positions if painful.

## Cardio Options

Low impact:

- Walking.
- Cycling.
- Elliptical.
- Swimming.
- Rowing, if technique is comfortable.

Higher impact:

- Running.
- Jump rope.
- Court sports.
- Intervals.

Choose low-impact options for beginners, higher bodyweight users, joint sensitivity, or high soreness.

## Warm-Ups and Cool-Downs

General warm-up:

- 5 to 10 minutes easy cardio or dynamic movement.

Specific warm-up:

- 1 to 3 lighter sets of the main movement.
- Gradually increase range, load, and intent.

Cool-down:

- Easy walking or cycling.
- Gentle stretching for worked areas.
- Breathing downshift if the session was intense.
