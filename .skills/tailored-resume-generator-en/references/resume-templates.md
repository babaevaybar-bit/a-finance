# Resume Templates and Output Patterns (English)

Use this reference when the user asks for a structured resume, resume shell, ATS-friendly template, bullet examples, or cover letter format.

## ATS-Friendly Resume Structure

```markdown
# [Candidate Name]

[City, Country] | [Email] | [Phone] | [LinkedIn] | [Portfolio/GitHub]

## Summary

[2-4 lines: target role, years/scope, strongest domain, strongest outcomes]

## Core Skills

- [Skill group]: [keywords]
- [Skill group]: [keywords]
- [Skill group]: [keywords]

## Professional Experience

### [Company] - [Title]
[Dates] | [Location/Remote]

- [Action + scope + method + measurable outcome]
- [Action + stakeholder/product/system + impact]
- [Action + tool/process + efficiency/revenue/quality/user outcome]

## Selected Projects

### [Project]

- [Problem solved]
- [What the candidate did]
- [Result or learning]

## Education

[Degree] - [Institution]

## Certifications / Publications / Awards

- [Optional]
```

## Summary Formulas

### Product / Business

`[Role] with [X years/scope] across [domain]. Known for [strength 1], [strength 2], and delivering [impact]. Seeking [target role] focused on [target outcome].`

### Engineering / Data

`[Engineer/Data role] experienced in [systems/tools/domain]. Built [type of systems] for [users/business area], improving [performance/reliability/cost/decision quality]. Strong in [technical strengths].`

### Operations / Customer / Support

`[Role] with experience improving [process/customer/business outcome]. Strengths include [workflow], [stakeholder management], and [metric/result].`

## Bullet Formula Library

### Impact Bullet

`[Action verb] [project/process/system] for [audience/scope], resulting in [metric/outcome].`

### Scale Bullet

`Managed [scope/volume/team/process] across [timeframe/business area], improving [quality/speed/cost/customer outcome].`

### Technical Bullet

`Built/optimized [system/tool/pipeline] using [technologies], enabling [business/user outcome].`

### Leadership Bullet

`Led [team/stakeholders/workstream] to [deliverable], aligning [groups] and achieving [outcome].`

### Ambiguous Metric Bullet

`Improved [process/outcome] by [method]; add baseline, metric, or business result if available. [verify metric]`

## Cover Letter Structure

```markdown
Dear [Hiring Team/Name],

I am interested in the [Role] position because [specific company/team/product reason tied to the JD].

In my recent work at [Company/Context], I [proof point 1]. This maps directly to your need for [JD need]. I also [proof point 2], which would help with [company/team goal].

What makes this role especially compelling is [specific motivation]. I would welcome the chance to discuss how my experience in [relevant strengths] can support [company outcome].

Best,
[Name]
```

## Review Rubric

| Area | What Good Looks Like |
|---|---|
| Target fit | Resume clearly aligns with JD priorities |
| Evidence | Claims are backed by outcomes, scope, or specifics |
| Keywords | Critical terms are present naturally |
| Readability | Recruiter can scan the resume in 30 seconds |
| Truthfulness | No unsupported or exaggerated claims |
| Seniority | Language matches role level |

## Verification Checklist

- All numbers come from user-provided material.
- All dates and chronology are consistent.
- All job titles match the candidate's real experience.
- All project descriptions can be explained by the candidate.
- No claims are fabricated, exaggerated, or unsupported.
