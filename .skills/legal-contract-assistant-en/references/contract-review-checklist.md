## Universal Contract Review

Check:

- Are the parties correctly identified?
- Is the contract type clear?
- Are effective date, term, and renewal mechanics clear?
- Are key obligations measurable?
- Are important definitions precise?
- Are notices and approval processes workable?
- Are remedies clear if the other side breaches?
- Does the contract align with the business deal?
- Are documents incorporated by reference available?
- Are order forms, exhibits, statements of work, DPAs, SLAs, or policies consistent with the main agreement?

Risks:

- Undefined key terms.
- Conflict between main agreement and attachments.
- Broad unilateral discretion.
- Missing acceptance process.
- Missing cure periods.
- Hidden auto-renewal.
- Overbroad compliance obligations.
- One-sided remedies.

## Commercial Terms

Review:

- Scope of services, deliverables, or products.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Dependencies and customer responsibilities.
- Exclusivity or minimum commitments.
- Volume commitments.
- Change order mechanics.
- Warranties and disclaimers.

High-risk patterns:

- Scope is broad but price is fixed.
- Acceptance is subjective or deemed accepted too quickly.
- Vendor can change service materially without customer consent.
- Customer must provide dependencies without clear timelines.
- Exclusivity blocks future business without clear value.

## Payment and Taxes

Review:

- Fees, invoicing schedule, payment due dates.
- Late fees and suspension rights.
- Expense reimbursement.
- Taxes, withholding, currency, and exchange-rate risk.
- Refunds, credits, and disputed invoices.

High-risk patterns:

- No right to dispute invoices.
- Payment due before acceptance.
- Broad suspension rights for minor disputes.
- Non-refundable fees despite vendor breach.
- Unlimited pass-through expenses.

## Term and Termination

Review:

- Initial term and renewal.
- Auto-renewal notice window.
- Termination for convenience.
- Termination for cause.
- Cure periods.
- Effects of termination.
- Transition assistance and data return.

High-risk patterns:

- Auto-renewal with narrow cancellation window.
- No termination right for uncured breach.
- Vendor can terminate convenience but customer cannot.
- Fees accelerate after termination.
- No post-termination transition support.

## Liability and Indemnity

Review:

- Liability cap amount.
- Cap basis: fees paid, annual fees, fixed amount.
- Exclusions from cap.
- Consequential damages waiver.
- Indemnity scope.
- Defense control and settlement consent.

Common buyer asks:

- Carve-outs for confidentiality breach, data breach, IP infringement, fraud, willful misconduct, gross negligence, payment obligations, and indemnity claims.
- Cap tied to at least 12 months of fees, sometimes higher for data/security risks.
- Indemnity for third-party IP claims and regulatory claims caused by vendor breach.

High-risk patterns:

- Liability cap too low relative to exposure.
- Indemnity is one-sided.
- Consequential damage waiver eliminates meaningful remedies.
- No duty to defend.
- Settlement can impose obligations without consent.

## Confidentiality

Review:

- Definition of confidential information.
- Exclusions.
- Use restrictions.
- Disclosure to affiliates, contractors, advisors.
- Protection standard.
- Duration.
- Return or destruction.

High-risk patterns:

- Confidentiality term is too short for trade secrets.
- Recipient can disclose too broadly.
- No injunctive relief or equitable remedy.
- No obligation to return or delete information.

## Intellectual Property

Review:

- Pre-existing IP ownership.
- Work product ownership.
- License grants.
- Feedback rights.
- Open-source restrictions.
- AI-generated or model-training rights, if relevant.

High-risk patterns:

- Vendor owns custom deliverables paid for by customer.
- Broad right to use customer data, brand, feedback, or content.
- No infringement indemnity.
- Ambiguous ownership of improvements.

## Data Protection and Security

Review:

- Personal data roles: controller, processor, business, service provider.
- Data processing addendum.
- Subprocessors.
- Security measures.
- Breach notification timing.
- Data location and transfers.
- Data return and deletion.
- Audit and security reports.

High-risk patterns:

- No DPA despite personal data processing.
- Breach notice is delayed or discretionary.
- Vendor can use data for unrelated purposes.
- No subprocessors control.
- No deletion obligation after termination.

## Service Levels and Support

Review:

- Uptime SLA.
- Support channels and response times.
- Maintenance windows.
- Service credits.
- Escalation.
- Disaster recovery.

High-risk patterns:

- SLA has no remedy.
- Credits are exclusive remedy for severe outage.
- Maintenance can happen anytime.
- No support commitment for critical incidents.

## Compliance and Regulatory Obligations

Review:

- Applicable laws.
- Anti-bribery and sanctions.
- Export control.
- Privacy laws.
- Industry-specific laws.
- Accessibility, if relevant.
- AI, cybersecurity, financial, healthcare, education, or employment compliance if relevant.

High-risk patterns:

- User must comply with laws caused by counterparty operations.
- Broad audit or certification obligations without scope limits.
- No sanctions or anti-corruption commitments.

## Audit, Records, and Reporting

Review:

- Audit rights.
- Notice periods.
- Frequency limits.
- Confidentiality during audit.
- Cost allocation.
- Remediation obligations.

High-risk patterns:

- Unlimited audit rights.
- No confidentiality on audit results.
- Broad access to systems or sensitive data.
- No remediation deadline.

## Assignment and Change of Control

Review:

- Assignment consent.
- Affiliate assignment.
- M&A assignment.
- Competitor transfer restrictions.

High-risk patterns:

- Counterparty can assign to any third party.
- Customer cannot assign in M&A.
- No protection against assignment to competitor.

## Governing Law and Disputes

Review:

- Governing law.
- Venue and jurisdiction.
- Arbitration or litigation.
- Injunctive relief.
- Attorneys' fees.
- Limitation periods.

High-risk patterns:

- Unfavorable or impractical venue.
- Mandatory arbitration with high cost.
- One-sided fee shifting.
- Waiver of equitable relief for confidentiality or IP issues.

## Missing or Abnormal Terms

Flag when missing:

- Parties, date, scope, pricing, term.
- Termination rights.
- Liability cap.
- Confidentiality.
- IP ownership.
- Data protection.
- Indemnity.
- Governing law.
- Notices.
- Order of precedence.

Flag as abnormal:

- Unlimited unilateral modification.
- Retroactive obligations.
- Perpetual payment commitments.
- Non-compete or exclusivity beyond deal scope.
- Assignment without consent.
- Mandatory public logo/reference rights.
- Broad audit rights.
- Waiver of all claims.

## Review Patterns by Contract Type

### NDA

Focus on definition of confidential information, permitted disclosures, duration, residual knowledge, compelled disclosure, return/destruction, and non-solicit or non-compete language hidden inside the NDA.

### SaaS Agreement

Focus on uptime, support, security, privacy, data use, subprocessors, service changes, renewal, suspension, liability, data export, and termination assistance.

### MSA/SOW

Focus on order of precedence, scope, change orders, acceptance, fees, timelines, dependencies, IP ownership, warranties, indemnity, and termination.

### Vendor/Procurement Agreement

Focus on service levels, audit rights, compliance, insurance, data security, indemnity, liability cap, continuity, subcontractors, and termination.

### DPA

Focus on processing instructions, subprocessors, data transfer, security measures, breach notice, deletion, audit, data subject requests, and regulatory cooperation.

### Partnership Agreement

Focus on roles, economics, exclusivity, branding, IP, customer ownership, data sharing, termination, non-circumvention, and dispute resolution.
